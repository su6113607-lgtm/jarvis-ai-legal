Privacy Policy

JARVIS AI

Effective date: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

JARVIS AI is a personal AI assistant currently in testing and development. It is designed to help authorized users manage accounting entries, business records, GST calculations and reports through WhatsApp.

2. Information We Process

Depending on how you use the service, JARVIS AI may process your WhatsApp phone number, WhatsApp profile name, messages you send, and accounting information such as sales, purchases, expenses and GST details.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to understand your requests, generate responses, prepare accounting entries, calculate totals and generate reports.

4. Third-Party Services

JARVIS AI uses the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta to receive and send messages.

Message content may be sent to the OpenAI API to interpret requests and generate responses. Meta and OpenAI process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Storage

Accounting records are stored in the JARVIS AI accounting workbook on the configured computer and OneDrive folder. Backup copies may also be created in the configured backup folder.

Temporary conversation and confirmation information may be held in application memory while the service is running.

6. Data Retention

Accounting records are retained until they are deleted by the service operator or are no longer required for the service. Backup copies may also contain previously saved records.

8. Security
