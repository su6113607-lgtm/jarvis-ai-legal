How to request deletion

Send an email to: su6113607@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number, including country code.
  • Your name, if applicable.

What information can be deleted?

  • WhatsApp-related user information held by JARVIS AI.

Verification and processing

We may contact you to verify that you are authorized to request deletion of the relevant information. Once verified, the request will be reviewed and eligible information under our control will be deleted manually.

Some information may need to be retained where required by applicable law or legitimate recordkeeping obligations. Any such retention will be explained where applicable.
